# UDYAM AI — Data Source Registry

## 1. Public Government Datasets (Public / Open Access)
- **Udyam Registration Portal Data**: Categorization thresholds (Micro: Turnover < ₹5Cr, Small: < ₹50Cr, Medium: < ₹250Cr).
- **GeM (Government e-Marketplace)**: Public tender notices, L1 price trends, vendor onboarding categories.
- **PMEGP / CGTMSE / PM Vishwakarma / ZED Schemes**: Official Ministry of MSME guidelines, subsidy limits, eligibility rules.
- **GST Portal Notifications**: CBIC tax circulars, rate lists, GSTR filing schedules.

## 2. Customer-Consented Private Data (Strict Tenant Scope)
- **Sales Registers**: POS logs, sales invoices, Tally/Vyapar exports.
- **Purchase & Inventory**: Stock counts, PO history, supplier lead times.
- **Financial Statements**: Bank statements via Account Aggregators (Setu / Finvu), balance sheets, P&L.
- **Tax & Statutory Documents**: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B filings, ITR acknowledgments, DSC renewal dates.
- **Agreements**: Vendor agreements, lease contracts, B2B service contracts uploaded by user.

## 3. Public Market & External Signals
- **Search Trends**: Google Trends index for product categories (e.g., "baggy jeans", "brass valves").
- **Sector News Radar**: Aggregated RSS & News API signals filtered for MSME impact.
- **Festival & Cultural Calendar**: Regional & national Indian festival schedules with lead-time indicators (Dhanteras, Diwali, Eid, Holi, Back-to-school, Wedding seasons).
